Our Statement

The Heart of Our Heritage: A Canvas for Every Indian. We believe the future of India is shaped in the hands of our creators.

Our DIY kits are Born in India, Built by Indians and inspired by our culture. Crafted with locally sourced materials, they honor centuries of Indian artistry.

When You Choose Our Kits, You Are:

  • Supporting Indian labor
  • Strengthening our creative economy
  • Preserving artistic heritage
  • Becoming a creator of Indian art

Our Mission

To reignite Indian creativity by bringing locally crafted DIY kits into every home.

  • Supporting Atmanirbhar Bharat
  • Promoting Vocal for Local
  • Using 100% Indian materials
  • Reviving traditional craftsmanship

Our Vision

  • Every home becomes a creative studio
  • Every child builds with confidence
  • Indian art thrives in modern homes
  • Creativity shapes the future of India
